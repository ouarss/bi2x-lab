Reading the knobs

Each knob shows three numbers: the raw 16-bit position, the same position on the 0 – 1023 scale a check screen displays, and the revolutions travelled since this page connected.

One revolution is one full range. The counter wraps once per turn, which is why a reader has to sample fast: sample slowly and half a turn looks like half a turn backwards, since nothing distinguishes the two. Measured by counting wraps over five slow revolutions: 65357 counts each, within 0.3 % of 65536.

Turn a knob exactly five times and watch the revolution count: if it does not read 5, the reading is losing motion, and no amount of scaling downstream will put it back.
